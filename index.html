How Smart RSVP works

RSVPs that take care of themselves.

From the first tap to the last seat, Smart RSVP handles the replies so you can enjoy the planning.

1For guests

Guests just find their name.

They tap RSVP on your invitation and type their name as written. Small typos are fine, and nobody needs to sign up or download anything.

Typo-friendly searchNo appAny phone
2No surprises

Only the seats you give them.

FAQ

Good to know.

Which events do you make invitations for?

Weddings, debuts, christenings, birthdays, anniversaries and more. Each event gets its own settings.

How do I book?

Send us a message with your event, date and motif. We'll send you our packages and the reservation form, and your slot is secured once the down payment is in.

Can we request changes to the design?

Yes. We review the design with you and refine it until every detail reflects what you envisioned.

Do guests need an app or an account to RSVP?

No. They tap RSVP on your invitation and it opens on any phone. No downloads, no sign-ups, no passwords.

Can guests bring extra people?

Only if you allow it. You set the seats for each family or group, so there are no surprise plus-ones.

How do we see who's coming?

On your private dashboard. Live headcount, who hasn't replied, seating by table and a list you can download for your caterer. You can add or edit guests there too.

Can we update the invitation after we send it?

Yes. Because it's a website, changes show for every guest right away. No reprints.

Can we thank our guests after the event?

If you'd like. A thank-you email can go to every guest the day after your event. It's optional.
